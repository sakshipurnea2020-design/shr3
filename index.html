Frequently Asked Questions

No matching questions found — ask us directly.

Is Shreya Namankan really free for students?
Yes. Shreya Namankan charges absolutely nothing to students.
Which countries can I apply to?
We support applications to 15+ countries including United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, USA, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Ireland, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, Sweden, Switzerland, Denmark, Finland, Belgium, Austria, Czech Republic, Malaysia, and UAE.
Is IELTS mandatory for all universities?
Not always. Many partner universities offer IELTS waivers for students who have studied in English-medium institutions. We assess your eligibility and apply for the waiver on your behalf, saving you time and the IELTS exam cost.
Is it true that public universities in Germany are free?
Yes. Most public universities in Germany charge only a semester fee (150-350 EUR) for administration and transport. There is no tuition fee for international students at most state universities.
How do I get started?
Simply fill out the contact form on this page or send us a WhatsApp message at +91-8150035443. We will schedule a free profile evaluation call and guide you from there. No commitment required.
Is it safe to study abroad as an Indian student?
Yes, it is safe. Studying abroad is more than earning a degree; it is an investment in your future. With the right course, destination, and career roadmap, you can make informed decisions, develop in-demand skills, and move closer to your professional goals with confidence.
