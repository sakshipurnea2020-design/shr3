📚 The Basics

What Is an Education Loan?

An education loan is a financial facility designed to help students fund their higher education. Depending on the lender, course, university and applicant profile, the loan may cover expenses such as:

🎓Tuition and university fees
🏠Accommodation and living expenses
✈️Travel expenses
📚Books, equipment and other academic costs
📋Other approved education-related expenses

The loan is repaid according to the terms set by the respective lender, including the applicable interest rate and repayment period.

Why Consider an Education Loan?

Higher education can require a substantial investment. An education loan allows families to spread this cost over a longer period rather than paying the entire expense at once.

For eligible borrowers, applicable tax benefits may also be available on education-loan interest, subject to the prevailing tax laws and eligibility requirements.

⚠️ Students and families should consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to their circumstances.
